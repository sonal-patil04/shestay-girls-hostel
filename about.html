🏠 Hostel Rules & Safety Policy

🚪 Main gate closes at 10:30 PM. Late entry only with prior warden approval.
👥 Male visitors are not permitted beyond the lobby. Lobby visits between 10 AM–7 PM only.
📋 All visitors must submit valid photo ID at the security desk before entry.
🚬 Smoking, alcohol and drugs are strictly prohibited on the premises.
📹 CCTV footage is reviewed daily. Any suspicious activity is reported immediately.
🚨 Emergency contacts and panic button locations are displayed in every room and corridor.
📱 Parents can register for the daily safety check-in SMS service at the front desk.
🌙 Night warden does room rounds at 11 PM and 3 AM for safety verification.
